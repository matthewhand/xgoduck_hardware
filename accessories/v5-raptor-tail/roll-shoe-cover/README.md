# Lift-off roll-shoe cover

Both rounded runners are integral to this [replacement cover](tail-cover-with-runners.stl). Install the root yoke and tail on the cover, then slide the assembly onto the V1.1 rails until the latch seats. To remove the whole tail package, release the existing cover tongue and lift the cover toward the head. Both runners, the yoke and the attached tail travel together. Pulling only the root hinge pin removes just the tail. There is no separate shoe latch.

The revised runner roots cleared the separate yoke and both M3 fasteners. CAD checked the released cover through 76 mm of continuous travel on both the original fixed rails and the removable rail cartridges. The runners add an estimated 5.66 g of printed material. Use local support where the curved beams leave the bed. Physical latch force, runner strength and fingertip access need an unpowered fit check. The CAD slide check covers the enclosure and rails. Centre the head and keep wires clear while servicing the assembled robot.

In the flat-floor right-side test, the stock policies stood up in 3.26 s and walked another 2.45 m with the printed runner profile, removable cartridges and 167 mm, 90%-width curved tail. The test used one 90° start and a 12° reference. The Duck Lab before/after replay uses the earlier capsule approximation of the same two runners.

For normal use with board protection, slide on the [plain guard](../../v5-slide-on-backpack/removable-rails/print/plain-protective-cover.stl). For the closest rear profile to stock, also remove the two cartridges, leaving the recessed receiver.
